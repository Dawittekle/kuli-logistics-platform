Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, vehicle vetting, cargo security, and telephone booking.

Kulli uses a fully transparent, upfront pricing model with no roadside bargaining. The fare consists of a fixed base fare for your chosen vehicle class (e.g. 600 ETB for Small Pickup, 1,200 ETB for Isuzu NPR) plus a distance rate per kilometer calculated using actual Addis Ababa road network distances. You see the exact guaranteed price before confirming your booking.

Every driver on Kulli undergoes strict physical document verification, including their government-issued commercial driver's license, vehicle title (Libre), and Kebele ID. All trips are tracked with live GPS from pickup to drop-off. You inspect your goods upon arrival before approving payment release, and our dedicated operations support handles any dispute resolution.

Yes! You do not need a smartphone or internet connection to use Kulli. Simply call our central dispatch hotline at 8492. A Kulli logistics assistant will take your pickup and delivery details, confirm your guaranteed rate, and dispatch an available truck directly to your gate with SMS updates.

We provide comprehensive coverage across all 11 sub-cities of Addis Ababa: Bole, Kirkos, Yeka, Arada, Addis Ketema (Merkato), Lideta, Kolfe Keranio, Gullele, Nifas Silk-Lafto, Akaki-Kality, and Lemi Kura. We also support regional inter-zone transport to surrounding Oromia special zones including Dukem, Bishoftu, Sebeta, and Burayu.

We support flexible payment options: you can pay cash directly to the driver upon delivery inspection, or complete an instant mobile money transfer via Telebirr or CBE Birr. In both cases, you receive an instant digital receipt and trip waybill on your phone.

You can register directly via the Kulli Driver App or visit our driver onboarding centers at Mexico Square or Bole Medhanialem. Bring your valid commercial driver's license, vehicle title (Libre), and Kebele ID. Most driver partners are verified and receiving cargo requests within 24 hours.
